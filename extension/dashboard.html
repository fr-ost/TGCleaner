Connect to the TG Cleaner service

The extension talks to the TG Cleaner service, which talks to Telegram. Run it on this computer, or use your hosted copy (e.g. on Railway).

  1. On this computer: run ./start.sh (macOS/Linux) or start.bat (Windows) in the project folder and keep that window open. Address: http://127.0.0.1:8765, no token.
  2. Hosted: enter your service's https:// address and the TGC_ACCESS_TOKEN you set there.

Connect your Telegram account

Your number, login code and password go only to the local service and on to Telegram. They are never stored or logged.
